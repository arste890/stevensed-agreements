# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Anyone StevensED asks to sign: parents and guardians (enrollment, health, media and records forms for their students), adult students and clients, staff and instructors, and outside parties such as vendors and venues. Most arrive from an emailed signing link, often on a phone, without an account. Staff (admin, editor, viewer roles) and families with StevensED accounts sign in through auth.stevensed.org. Students under 13 never sign (FRM-102, DocuSeal checklist).

## Product Purpose

StevensED Agreements (agreements.stevensed.org) is StevensED's self-hosted document signing service, a modified DocuSeal operated by the Office of Legal Operations and Institutional Compliance. It sends agreements for signature, records an audit trail, and returns completed copies. Success: a signer understands within seconds that they should open the emailed link, can sign on any device, and trusts that the process is StevensED's own and legally effective.

## Positioning

Institution-wide (all StevensED programs, including The Music Academy). Signatures are governed by StevensED's Electronic Records and Signature Consent (FRM-102): same effect as a handwritten signature under the Utah Uniform Electronic Transactions Act and 15 U.S.C. 7001; free paper copies on request; completed copy to every signer.

## Constraints

- Legal links: stevensed.org Privacy, Cookies, Terms, Disclaimer, Acceptable Use; Policy Library (policies.stevensed.org) including FRM-102, STU-006, INF-006, INF-002; an Accessibility statement; Legal notices (AGPLv3 attribution for DocuSeal).
- DocuSeal attribution stays on the Legal notices page (AGPLv3 section 7(b)).
- Brand assets come from StevensED-Website (logo, wordmark, photography) and the Policy Library (self-hosted Cormorant Garamond and Inter, OFL).
- Institutional, measured voice; no em dashes.
